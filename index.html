About

Meet Randyne Neo

Co-founder of JLD · PropNex Realty

After three years at PwC and KPMG specialising in Personal Income Tax, Randyne found himself wanting to pursue something more dynamic. Inspired by his partner's journey in real estate, he made the switch and is now two years into the industry.

Drawing on his financial background, Randyne specialises in deep-diving into market data and numbers to help clients navigate residential, commercial and industrial property decisions.

His proudest achievement is building JLD alongside June, and watching the team learn and grow together. Whether he's working with clients or teammates, Randyne believes in keeping things genuine, putting people first and doing what's right for them.

“Focus on building genuine relationships, and the results will take care of themselves.”
